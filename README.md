# PTS-FlightApp
Flight Details and Precision Timing Schedule (PTS) - Interactive web application for flight operations management

## v1.22 beta: transfer one tab at a time

Open the **separate beta on both devices**: https://jackyyouyang-commits.github.io/PTS-FlightApp/beta/PTS_FlightApp.html

The stable page remains v1.22 and is unchanged. Beta has its own saved-flight/legacy-key namespace and PWA identity; it never automatically reads, migrates, clears or overwrites stable storage. To avoid re-entering a laptop flight, first **Save** in stable, then in beta select **Connect > Seed this beta from stable > Copy saved flight from stable v1.22**. This explicitly reads a detached copy of the complete stable snapshot and asks before replacing the entire beta flight. It does not fall back to stale standalone fields. A stable `.pts` file can also be read locally in this section. No content is uploaded by either action.

Select the desired tab, then **Connect**. The **Send only this tab** selector defaults to the current data tab and can be changed in the dialog. On the receiver use **Connect > Scan QR Code to Connect**. The sender shows the actual outgoing part count and minimum cycle after preparing that tab; the receiver shows its name, total parts, received parts and minimum cycle from the first frame. Stop/resume retains collected parts; closing Connect discards them. Once every part passes the integrity and shape checks, confirm replacement of the **named tab only**. Empty fields, image lists and PDF slots explicitly clear that selected tab's corresponding content. There is no partial import before completion, and a storage failure prevents the tab merge.

All 13 visible data tabs have a transfer option: **Flight Info** (all arrival/departure fields and passenger-loading rows), **PTS Schedule** (both actual-time tables and reminder acknowledgements), **Staff Acceptance**, **Arrival PTM**, **Arrival BNL**, **Arrival Disruption PPM**, **ITD Tag Card**, **Arrival Offload**, **Departure Onload**, **Arrival Crew List**, **Departure Gen Dec**, **Agent Positions**, and **Ramp Roster**. Each operational tab includes its text, rich formatting, pasted images and its PDF slot where present. **Summary** is derived from Flight Info and PTS Schedule, not a separate store; send those two tabs to update it. A legacy departure-crew field has no visible tab in this version; nonempty legacy content there is rejected during full-flight seeding rather than silently discarded.

Set the same flight number **and full date including year** on both devices (for example `CX888` and `10 Sep 2026`; day-first `10/09/2026` and ISO dates are also accepted). Case, whitespace and flight-number leading zeros are normalized. An empty receiver initializes the identity only. A receiver with any flight content must already have a valid matching identity; another flight is rejected, never silently replaced. Send Flight Info first for schedule targets based on the same ETA/STD. Tab imports refresh targets/countdowns but do not auto-write another tab's fields: the persisted Flight Info values remain authoritative on subsequent reloads. Normal manual edits/logging still perform the shipped auto-field updates and 95-minute turnaround calculation.

Beta sends a distinct `#ptsb=1...` optical protocol that stable scanners cannot mistake for a whole-flight replacement. Small tabs fit one static QR; image/PDF-heavy tabs may still take many parts and several cycles. The existing 650-character chunks, 1.1-second interval, 320px display and local viewing-copy optimization remain unchanged. Every selected image and PDF page is retained (original attachments remain an explicit fallback). No backend, hosted-flight link, WebRTC, device-to-device network transfer or file upload is involved; flight content travels only in QR images. Opening the app and loading PDF.js can require internet access, but content is processed locally.

The beta service worker caches only known beta assets in its own cache and never deletes stable caches. Offline beta works after successful installation, including shared root vendor/icon assets. **Known stable-worker limitation:** a future activation of the unchanged root stable worker deletes other caches, including beta's. If that happens, reopen beta online to rebuild its offline cache. PDF.js still needs an initial online load for PDF rendering/optimization; use original attachments explicitly if unavailable.

## Transfer to an iPad without file uploads

1. Open the published app on both devices: https://jackyyouyang-commits.github.io/PTS-FlightApp/PTS_FlightApp.html
2. On the laptop, fill in the flight and operational tabs, then select **Connect**. Wait while the app prepares smaller, readable attachment copies and calculates the QR sequence.
3. On the iPad, select **Connect > Scan QR Code to Connect**, allow camera access, and point the camera at the laptop's QR code. Use the scanner inside this app, not the iPad Camera app.
4. Keep scanning the automatically cycling codes until **Flight Data Successfully Retrieved** appears. Missing parts are picked up on the next cycle; duplicates and out-of-order parts are supported.

The transfer includes Flight Info, PTS actual times, all operational text tabs, rich formatting, pasted images, and imported PDFs. Data is imported only after the complete sequence passes its integrity check, then saved immediately on the receiving device. It remains compatible with the single- and multi-part QR formats introduced in `43e25dc`.

**A single QR is intentionally static.** When all current data fits in 650 compressed characters, the status reads **QR 1 of 1**. Larger payloads automatically cycle through 650-character parts every 1.1 seconds, with the same 320px QR display. Parts are chunks of the complete flight, not one QR per tab.

**QR transfer uses viewing copies, not smaller desktop originals.** Plain text, table data and rich formatting are preserved; no tab, image or PDF page is discarded or cropped. Suitable images are reduced to at most 1600 pixels wide (long screenshots retain their full height at that scale). Opaque images may use JPEG quality 0.8; transparent images retain transparency, and vector/animated images remain intact. PDFs are rebuilt as readable page images with every page and its aspect ratio preserved; these PDF viewing copies do not retain selectable/searchable text. All processing is on the sending device. PDF rendering uses the app's existing PDF.js loader and may need internet access to load that library, but flight content is not uploaded. Desktop attachments and saved state are unchanged.

The dialog compares original and outgoing payload sizes and shows the outgoing QR part count and **minimum full-cycle time**. For source payloads up to 8 MB, it also compares original QR part counts and keeps the smaller complete sequence; larger source payloads skip that extra compression pass to limit memory use. Allow additional cycles for missed scans: for example, 1,000 parts require at least **18 minutes 20 seconds per cycle** and can still take much longer to receive completely. This is not an instant transfer. Individual attachments are kept unchanged when their viewing copies would be larger. If preparation fails, the dialog names the failed attachment and offers an explicit **Use original attachments (slower)** fallback; nothing is silently omitted. Closing the dialog or switching to the scanner cancels preparation.

The Connect dialog shows how many operational tabs on this device contain content. If it says no flight data is loaded, populate the laptop app before sending, or use the scanner on the receiving device. The laptop only displays the QR and does not need camera access. Cancelling scanning or encountering a camera error returns to the outgoing QR display and resumes any multi-part sequence.

No file picker, download, upload, or account is required for QR transfer. The iPad must permit camera access to the HTTPS app; the app cannot override company device restrictions. If storage is full or blocked, **Flight Data Retrieved - Not Saved** warns that the received flight is not yet saved; keep the page open and retry **Save** when storage is available. Complete-flight ten-minute autosave and save-before-suspension remain in place.
